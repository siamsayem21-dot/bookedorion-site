Questions

No. Molly is the core. Turn on the included services you want, and add the paid ones only if they fit your shop.

Who sets it up?+

We do. You answer a short checklist and listen to a test call. Changes are a text away and done by the next business day.

When do customer texts start?+

Once your number is registered for business texting, which we handle during setup. Until then, Molly still answers and books calls.

Never. Reminders and follow-ups only go to your own customers, and tune-up reminders only to customers who agreed to hear from you.
