The service

BookedOrion provides an AI receptionist (“Molly”) that answers calls for your business, books appointments on your calendar and sends you call summaries and alerts.

Free 14-day trial

We set up the service and run it free for 14 days. There is no setup fee. After the trial, you can choose a plan or stop.

Plans and billing

  • Pay-per-Job: $79 a month plus $29 for each booked job, never more than $449 in a month. If Molly books no jobs in your first paid month, that month is free.
  • Flat: $249 a month with unlimited booked jobs.
  • Night-only: Pay-per-Job with a $49 monthly base, covering nights, weekends and holidays only.
  • Plans are month to month. There is no long-term contract.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
  • Each plan includes 400 call minutes a month. We text you when you reach 80% (320 minutes). One busy month is fine. If you go over two months in a row, we agree on a bigger plan together. No surprise bills.
  • Spam and wrong-number calls are never counted.
  • Payment is by a secure invoice link. Molly never takes payment details on a call.

What counts as a booked job

  • A booked job is a new appointment from a call Molly handled. You confirm it with one tap in the text summary. If you don’t reply within 48 hours, it counts as booked. The call recording and summary are the record.
  • If you mark a booking as not booked, you pick a reason (spam, duplicate, outside your service area, not a real job, or customer cancelled). We check the recording before removing the charge.
  • Cancellations are free when there is a record: the customer called or texted Molly, or you send us the call recording or a screenshot of the text. The cancellation must be before the appointment time and within 7 days.
  • A no-show, or a customer who declines at the door, still counts, because the appointment was made.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate business information: hours, fees, service area and emergency contacts.
  • Keep your emergency phone numbers up to date and reachable.
  • Use the service in line with the laws that apply to your business, including rules on call recording and text messaging.

Limits of an AI receptionist

Molly is an AI assistant. She can misunderstand callers or make mistakes. She is not an emergency service and does not replace 911, your gas utility or your own judgment. Review your call summaries and tell us about any problem so we can fix the script.

Payments over the phone

Molly does not collect card numbers or other payment details by phone.

Text messaging program

  • Message frequency varies with your call volume.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text STOP to cancel at any time. After you send STOP, we will send one message to confirm you are unsubscribed.
  • Text HELP for help, or email siam@bookedorion.com.
