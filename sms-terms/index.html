SMS Terms

Last updated October 21, 2026

These terms apply to text messages sent by BookedOrion.

Program

Program name: BookedOrion Alerts

What you receive: call summaries, booking confirmations and urgent-call alerts about calls Molly answers for your business. We do not send marketing texts.

Who receives them: business owners and staff who sign up during setup and give consent to receive these texts.

Opting in

You opt in during setup by giving us the mobile number(s) to alert and agreeing to receive text messages from BookedOrion. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Frequency and cost

  • Message frequency varies. It depends on how many calls Molly answers for you.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

  • Text HELP for help, or email siam@bookedorion.com.
  • Text STOP to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming you are unsubscribed. You can rejoin by texting START.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Read our Privacy Policy.
