Your rules

You decide what counts as urgent

Not every late call is an emergency. During setup, you tell us how to handle each kind.

Gas smell or carbon monoxide

Safety first. Molly tells the caller to get outside and call 911 and the gas company from outside. She doesn’t book a routine visit.

Urgent for your shop

You choose the words that make a call urgent, like no heat with an elderly person at home. Molly follows the rule you set and the call is marked urgent in your text.

Everything else

Booked into the first open slot on your calendar, usually the next morning. You wake up to a list, not a voicemail box.

Molly is an AI assistant, not an emergency service. She doesn’t replace 911, your gas utility or your judgment.

Why it matters

A missed call at night is often a job for someone else

A homeowner with no heat at 10 PM may not wait for a call back in the morning. If they can’t reach you, the next shop is one tap away.

If you run Google Local Services Ads, Google’s own help page lists your responsiveness as a ranking factor and says Missed calls may negatively affect your responsiveness. Answering after hours is one way to miss fewer. Google decides rankings, so we never promise a position.

Source: Google Local Services Ads Help, “About ad rankings”

Will callers know they’re talking to an AI?+

Yes. Molly says she’s an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded, at the start of every call.

Do I keep my phone number?+

Yes. Your calls forward to Molly when you’re closed. To turn her off, you cancel the forwarding.

What does Molly do with a gas smell or carbon monoxide call?+

She tells the caller to get outside and call 911 and the gas company from outside. She doesn’t book a routine visit, and the call is marked urgent in your text.

Does she cover weekends and holidays?+

Yes. Night-only covers nights, weekends and holidays. You set the hours during setup. To change them later, send us a text and it’s done by the next business day.

Which calendar does she book on?+

Your Google or Outlook calendar. She doesn’t connect directly to ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro yet.

Does Molly quote prices?+

Only the diagnostic or trip fee you approve. She never guesses a repair price.

Does Molly speak Spanish?+

Not yet. We’ll only offer it once it passes our tests.

Are spam and wrong-number calls billed?+

Never.
