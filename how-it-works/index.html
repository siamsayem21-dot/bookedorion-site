ONE CALL, STEP BY STEP

What happens when a homeowner calls at 9:47 PM.

1
AnswerFirst few seconds
  1. 1Greets and discloses

    “Thanks for calling Lone Star Heating & Air. This is Molly, the AI assistant. This call may be recorded.”

  2. 2Listens to the problem

    No cooling, no heat, a noise, a leak, a quote. She asks a follow-up if it’s unclear.

  3. 3Checks for an emergency

    Hears one of your emergency words? She follows your rules and rings you. Gas or CO: “Please get outside and call 911.”

2
QualifyAbout a minute
  1. 4Takes the details

    Name, callback number, address, type of system, anyone at risk from heat or cold.

  2. 5Checks your area

    ZIP outside your service area? She says so politely instead of booking it.

  3. 6Mentions your fee

    States your diagnostic or trip fee before booking. No surprises at the door.

3
BookBefore hanging up
  1. 7Books the visit

    Offers open slots from your calendar. The caller picks one.

  2. 8Reads it back

    “Maria, 214 Oak Lane in Plano, AC blowing warm air, Wednesday 8 AM. Did I get that right?”

  3. 9Texts you

    Seconds later you get the summary. Recording and transcript are saved.

QUESTIONS

Setup questions owners ask

Do I need new software?+

No. Bookings go on the Google or Outlook calendar you already use, and details come to you by text and email.

What if I use ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?+

Every booking arrives with all the details, ready for your team to add in seconds. Ask us about a direct integration.

What if Molly isn’t sure?+

She takes a clear message and tells the caller you’ll call back. You get it by text right away.

Can I change the script later?+

Yes. Email us what to change and we update it, usually the same day.

Can I hear every call?+

Yes. Every call has a summary, a recording and a transcript.
