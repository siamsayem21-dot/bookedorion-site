ONE CALL, STEP BY STEP

What happens when a homeowner calls at 9:47 PM.

This is the order Molly follows on a normal service call. We set up every step with you before she goes live.

  1. 1
    Greets and discloses

    “Thanks for calling Lone Star Heating & Air. This is Molly, the AI assistant. This call may be recorded. How can I help?”

  2. 2
    Listens to the problem

    The caller explains in their own words: no cooling, no heat, a noise, a leak, a quote. Molly asks a follow-up if something is unclear.

  3. 3
    Checks for an emergency

    If the caller says one of your emergency words (gas, smoke, CO alarm, no heat with an elderly person), Molly follows your rules. For gas or CO she tells the caller to get outside and call 911, then rings you.

  4. 4
    Takes the details

    Name, callback number, service address and ZIP, what kind of system it is, and whether anyone at home is at risk from the heat or cold.

  5. 5
    Checks your service area

    If the ZIP is outside your area, Molly says so politely instead of booking a job you can’t take.

  6. 6
    Mentions your fee

    If you charge a diagnostic or trip fee, Molly states it before booking, so there are no surprises at the door.

  7. 7
    Books the visit

    She offers open slots from your calendar and books the one the caller picks.

  8. 8
    Reads it back

    “So that’s Maria at 214 Oak Lane in Plano, AC blowing warm air, Wednesday at 8 AM. Did I get that right?”

  9. 9
    Texts you

    Seconds after the call, you get a summary by text and email. The recording and transcript are saved.

SETUP

We do the setup. You answer a few questions.

  1. Day 1
    Short form

    Name, company and email. That’s all we need to start.

  2. Within 1 business day
    Setup questions

    Your hours, service area ZIPs, fees, emergency words, on-call number and which calendar to book on.

  3. Next
    We build and test

    We write Molly’s script for your shop, then you hear a test call. Nothing goes live until you say it sounds right.

  4. Go live
    Forward your calls

    You set your phone to forward missed or after-hours calls to Molly. We walk you through it on the phone. Your number stays the same.

  5. Day 14
    You decide

    If Molly didn’t book a job, you pay nothing. If she did, pick a plan or walk away.
