Questions owners ask

      

        
Will callers know it’s an AI?+
Yes. Molly says she’s an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.

        
Does she replace my office staff?+
No. Your team still answers first. Molly only takes the calls they miss.

        
Do I change my phone number?+
No. You keep your number and your Google listing. Unanswered calls forward to Molly.

        
What if a caller has a real emergency?+
You pick the emergency words. When Molly hears one, she rings your phone first.

        
What does the pilot cost?+
Nothing if Molly doesn’t book a job in 14 days. After that, pick a plan or walk away.

        
Is there a contract?+
No. Month to month. Cancel anytime.

        
What if I get more calls than my plan?+
We text you before you get close. Then you choose: move up a plan or pay a small fee per extra call, agreed with you before you start. No surprise bills.

        
Does it work with ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?+
Every booking is sent to you by text and email with all the details, ready to add. Ask us about direct integration.

        
I’m not good with tech. Is that okay?+
Yes. We set everything up for you. You only read the texts Molly sends.

        
Can I listen to the calls?+
Yes. You can read a summary of every call and listen to the recording.

        
What if Molly gets something wrong?+
Tell us and we fix her script, usually the same day. If she isn’t sure, she takes a message and you call back.
