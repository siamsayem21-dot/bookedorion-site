More answers on How it works

    
How is Molly different from other AI receptionists?+
Most AI receptionist apps are built for any business and you set them up yourself. Molly is built only for HVAC, a real person sets her up for your shop, and your team can still answer first.
Can my team still answer first?+
Yes. Your phone rings as usual. If nobody picks up in about 10–15 seconds, or the line is busy, the call goes to Molly. After hours, she can answer every call. You choose, and you can switch anytime.
Will callers know it’s an AI?+
Yes. Molly says she’s an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.
Do I change my phone number?+
No. You keep your number and your Google listing. Unanswered calls forward to Molly.
What if a caller has a real emergency?+
You pick the emergency words. When Molly hears one, she tells the caller how to stay safe and rings your phone first.
What does it cost to try?+
Nothing. The first 14 days are free, with no setup fee and no contract. On Pay-per-Job, if Molly books no jobs in your first paid month, that month is free too.
What counts as a booked job?+
A new appointment from a call Molly handled, which you confirm with one tap in the text summary. Spam and wrong-number calls never count. If the customer cancels and there’s a record of it, you aren’t charged.
Does it work with ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?+
Every booking is sent to you by text and email with all the details, ready to add. There’s no direct integration yet.
What if Molly gets something wrong?+
Tell us and we fix her script by the next business day. If she isn’t sure, she takes a message and you call back.
