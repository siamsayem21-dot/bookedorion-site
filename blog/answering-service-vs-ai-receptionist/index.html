Answering service vs. AI receptionist: an honest comparison for HVAC shops

Live answering services and AI receptionists both answer your phone. Here is how they compare on cost, booking, emergencies and caller experience.

Siam AhmedFounder, BookedOrion

If your phone goes unanswered on nights, weekends or busy afternoons, you have two common options: pay a live answering service, or use an AI receptionist. Both have a place. Here’s a straight comparison.

How each one works

A live answering service routes your calls to a shared team of receptionists. They follow your script, take messages and, on some plans, book appointments.

An AI receptionist is software that answers in a natural voice, asks the caller questions and can book directly onto your calendar. A good one says it’s an AI and hands off to a human when needed.

Cost: per minute vs. flat monthly

Many live services charge by the minute. As one example, published pricing for one well-known US virtual receptionist service ranged from $245 a month for 50 minutes to $705 a month for 200 minutes, with extra minutes billed at roughly $4–5 each (as reported in December 2025; check current prices with the provider).

That matters for HVAC because call volume isn’t steady. When the first heat wave hits, calls jump, and a per-minute bill jumps with them.

AI receptionists are usually priced per month with a call allowance, so a busy week costs about the same as a quiet one.

Where a live service still wins

  • Callers who strongly prefer a human from the first second.
  • Complicated situations that don’t fit any script.
  • Businesses that only need messages taken, not jobs booked.

Where an AI receptionist wins

  • Every call answered right away, even when ten come in at once.
  • The job gets booked, not just written down for tomorrow.
  • Predictable monthly cost, even in July.
  • A text summary of every call on your phone.

Five questions to ask any provider

  1. Does it book the appointment, or only take a message?
  2. What happens on a gas smell or no-heat call with an elderly caller?
  3. How does it pass an upset caller to a real person?
  4. What does a 2× busier month cost me?
  5. Can I listen to recordings and read summaries of every call?
