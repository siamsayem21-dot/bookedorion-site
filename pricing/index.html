The $29

What counts as a booked job

You only see the $29 for real appointments, and every one has a call recording behind it.

Counts

  • A new appointment from a call Molly handled
  • You confirm it with one tap in the text summary
  • No reply within 48 hours counts as booked
  • A no-show, or a customer who says no at the door

Never counts

  • Spam and wrong-number calls
  • Duplicates, or callers outside your service area
  • Calls that weren’t a real job
  • Cancellations with a record, made before the visit and within 7 days

Mark a booking as “not booked” in the text and pick a reason. We check the recording before removing the charge. Full rules are in our Terms.

Questions

Pricing questions

How much does BookedOrion cost?+

Two plans. Pay-per-Job is $79 a month plus $29 for each booked job, and never more than $449 in a month. Flat is $249 a month with unlimited booked jobs. Both include 400 call minutes a month and start with a free 14-day trial.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?+

No. Setup is free and we do it for you. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.

How does the free trial work?+

We set Molly up for your shop and run her free for 14 days. After the trial, you choose a plan or stop. There’s nothing to cancel if you stop.

What if Molly books nothing in my first month?+

On Pay-per-Job, if Molly books no jobs in your first paid month, that month is free.

What counts as a booked job?+

A new appointment from a call Molly handled. You confirm it with one tap in the text summary. If you don’t reply within 48 hours, it counts as booked. The call recording and summary are the record.

What if a customer cancels or doesn’t show up?+

Cancellations are free when there’s a record: the customer called or texted Molly, or you send us the call recording or a screenshot of the text. It must be before the appointment and within 7 days. A no-show still counts, because the appointment was made.

Do spam and wrong-number calls count?+

Never. They don’t count as booked jobs and they’re never billed.

What happens if we use more than 400 minutes?+

We text you at 80% (320 minutes). One busy month is fine. If you go over two months in a row, we agree on a bigger plan together. No surprise bills.

Which plan should I pick?+

Not sure? Start with Pay-per-Job. Each month we show you which plan would have cost you less, and you can switch.

We only need nights and weekends. Is there a plan for that?+

Yes. Night-only is $49 a month plus $29 per booked job. Your own team answers during the day, and Molly covers nights, weekends and holidays.

How do I pay?+

By a secure invoice link each month. Molly never takes card numbers or payment details on a call.
